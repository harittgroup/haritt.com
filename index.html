About us

Why we exist.

Powerful technology is everywhere, but most of it is built for experts. Ordinary people and small teams are left with tools that are complicated, expensive, or both.

Haritt Group was started to change that. We take what AI can do and turn it into products that anyone can open, understand in a minute, and use every day: to plan, to learn, to grow and to run their work.

We are an independent, founder-led company based in India, building for India first and for the world next.

Our mission

To make useful technology simple and accessible for everyone, from individuals to growing businesses.

Our vision

To become India’s most trusted home for simple, useful products that people rely on every day.

Our principles

How we build.

Useful before impressive

Every feature has to make someone’s day easier. If it does not help, it does not ship.

Simple by design

Plain words, clean screens and no learning curve. Anyone should get it in a minute.

Private by default

Your data belongs to you. We collect only what a feature needs and never sell it.

Honest and accessible

Free where we can be, clear about what we do, and built to work for people across India.
