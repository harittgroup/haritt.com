3.3

How Haritt Developers sounds

Verified from the site

We are

  • Plain and direct: short sentences, everyday words, no jargon without a one-line explanation
  • Measured: AI and every tool is used "only where there is a clear business purpose"
  • Honest: we say when a smaller scope is better and when we are not the right fit
  • Specific: a checklist, a step, an example from our own work
  • English first, simple Hinglish touches on Instagram and Facebook where the audience uses it

We are not

  • Hype: "best", "number one", "guaranteed leads", "10x your sales"
  • Jargon: "synergy", "disruptive", "full-funnel omnichannel"
  • Fake proof: invented clients, testimonials, before and after of work we did not do
  • Pushy: "limited offer", countdowns, "DM now"
  • Emoji walls, all caps or ten hashtags
Before

"We are a leading digital agency offering cutting-edge, end-to-end solutions to skyrocket your business growth!"

After

"Your website gets visitors but no enquiries? Check these 5 things on your homepage. Most fixes are small."

Lead playbook

How posts turn into enquiries, on purpose

A studio does not need millions of views. It needs the right few owners to think "these people explain things properly". Here is what earns that trust without hype.

5.1

Why an owner reaches out

Our reading
Clarity

"They explained it simply"

A checklist or a diagram that names a problem the owner already feels.

Ends with: "Save this and check your own site."

Proof

"They actually build things"

Real work, even our own: the HV Verse build, our site, process screens.

Ends with: "This is how we built it."

Honesty

"They told me what not to buy"

Posts that say when to skip an app, a chatbot or a redesign.

Ends with: "Start smaller. Here is how."

Ease

"It is easy to ask"

One clear next step and a reply that gives honest next steps.

Ends with: "Discuss your project: developers@haritt.com"

5.5

Ride the business year

Our reading check official dates every year

An owner thinks about a new site or campaign at predictable moments. Plan these a month ahead. Festival and sale dates change every year, so check an official calendar before scheduling.

5.6

Attract, but still us

Do
  • Useful first, proof second, ask last
  • Name the owner's problem in their words
  • One idea per post, made in a few formats
  • Answer every comment with a real answer
  • Say "I do not know" or "that is not our service" when true
Never
  • Fake clients, fake results, fake screenshots
  • Shaming another agency or a business's current site by name
  • Promises of rankings, leads or sales numbers
  • Countdown offers and "DM now" pressure
  • Copying another studio's carousel or case study

Growth and performance

How we grow, and how we know it works

Organic and local first. Useful posts, a steady rhythm, real conversations. For a studio, one good enquiry matters more than a thousand followers. Measured every Friday.

    Decisions

    What we need to decide

    The Haritt Developers plan is built on the site, the admin brand files and what you told us about the Google Business Profile. These points are open, each with a recommendation.

    Open

    Faces on camera

    A studio sells trust in people. Recommend: start faceless with screens and motion, add the team on camera for one video a month once everyone is comfortable. Draft

    Open

    Paid ads

    Recommend: organic only for the first 90 days, then test a small Facebook and Instagram budget aimed at local owners, with the enquiry tracking from 10.4 in place first. Draft

    Open

    A free website check

    A short, honest review of an owner's homepage is the natural first step. Recommend: offer it only if the team can deliver each one properly. Not an existing offer today. Draft

    Open

    Language mix

    Recommend: English first, with simple Hinglish on Instagram, Facebook and reels for local owners. LinkedIn and YouTube in English. Draft

    Open

    Showing our own work

    Without client work, our own sites and the HV Verse build are the proof. Confirm we can show real screens of HV Verse and say which parts were built with which tools. Draft

    Open

    Who posts and replies

    No fixed roles yet. Recommend: one person owns the weekly plan, one checks every post, and replies to enquiries go out the same day. Draft
