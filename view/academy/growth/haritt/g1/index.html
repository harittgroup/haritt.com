Being found by name

When someone hears Haritt Group, can they find haritt.com?

Searching a topic"web developer for small business"

Strangers, hard to win, not the Group's job.

vs
Searching the name"Haritt" or "Haritt Group"

People who already heard of you. This is the Group's job.

1
Haritt Group has nothing to sell on haritt.com. There is no product, no price and no sign-up form. So the usual idea of search marketing, where you try to appear when strangers type a problem into Google, is not the main job here. The main job is smaller and more realistic: when someone has already heard the name, from a founder, a client, a partner or the footer of one of your venture sites, and then types it into Google, they should land on haritt.com and not on someone else.
2
This is what people call brand search, or being found by name. The 14 October search study on file shows why it matters for you. Searching "Haritt" put haritt.com at number 6, mixed in with look-alikes: Harritt Group (a US company), Harit Developers (Pune), a person called Amine Harit, and Haritt Kawacch. That was one search, done before the rename, and no recheck is on file. So the honest picture is that your name is easy to confuse, and a visitor who is half sure of the spelling may reach the wrong company.
3
Three things decide whether this works: the page itself says clearly who Haritt Group is, search engines can read and index it, and other places on the web (profiles, directories, your own venture sites) point to haritt.com with the same description. The on-page part is already done. The rest is checking, and then slow, free tidy-up work.
Do you need this now? Yes, as the cheap first step. It costs no money and mostly means opening one free tool, reading what it shows, and making your profiles match. Nothing else in the Group's marketing can be judged until you know whether anyone searches the name at all.

Here is what your files say today. The on-page work is finished: every page has a keyword title, a canonical tag (a small line that tells Google which address is the main one for a page), and schema (hidden labels that tell search engines what the site is). The schema names the company as Haritt Group and lists the alternate names Haritt, Harit Group and Harit, plus seven profile links. The About page has a short question and answer, "Haritt, with two Ts". There is a sitemap with 10 URLs, a robots file and an llms.txt.

What is not on file is any real search data. Your task list says Search Console still needs doing, while the academy branding pages say it is set up. Both cannot be true, so the first job is to open it and see. Also not on file: whether Crunchbase, Product Hunt, AlternativeTo and Wellfound profiles exist. The study found no links from other sites pointing to haritt.com.

ACheck one: Search ConsoleOpen it and see whether haritt.com is already a property.

Go to Google Search Console while signed in with the Google account that owns the domain. Open the property drop-down at the top left. If haritt.com is listed, you are done with this check and can go to the next part. If it is not listed, nobody has verified it, and you will add it in the next part.

Readiness test: you can see haritt.com in that list and the Performance page opens without an error.

BCheck two: the name is consistentThe same company name and the same founding year everywhere.

Search the exact names Haritt, Haritt Group and Harit Group yourself, and write down what each page says about when the company started. The About page says founded in 2026, your own story says 2020, and a Justdial listing says established 2018 in Bharatpur. Search engines compare these details. If they disagree, they may treat them as different companies.

Readiness test: one founding line is chosen, and you can say it in one breath. This is a decision for you, not something to fix by guessing.

CCheck three: no office, so no map listingGoogle Business Profile is not the right tool for the Group.

A Google Business Profile is the free map listing for a shop or office. haritt.com shows no office address, so the Group does not qualify and should not have one. The profile that is prepared is for Haritt Developers as a service-area business. Do not create a second one for the Group. For the Group, the local job is only to keep names and details identical wherever they appear.

Readiness test: you can list every public listing (LinkedIn, Justdial, others) in one sheet with its name, year and link.

Ready to start? You are ready for Search Console as soon as you can sign in to the right Google account. If you cannot, the fix is to find out who registered the domain and its DNS, not to wait.

Made-up numbers to show how to read the screen. No real Search Console data exists in your files.

Search Console is Google's free report on how your site appears in Google Search. It only shows data from the day you verify, so the sooner it is set up, the sooner real numbers start. Menu names change from time to time, so treat the path below as a guide and look for the closest words if something moved.

1Add and verify the propertyDomain property is the cleanest choice.

Open Search Console, open the property drop-down, and choose to add a property. Pick the Domain type and type haritt.com. Google gives you a text record to add in the place where the domain's DNS is managed (the registrar or the service that holds the DNS). Paste it there, wait a few minutes, and press verify. If the domain option is awkward, the URL prefix type with a file or tag on the page also works, but it only covers the exact address you type.

2Submit the sitemapTell Google where your page list is.

In the left menu open Sitemaps (it sits under the Indexing group). In the box write sitemap.xml and submit. Status should change to Success, and it should show 10 discovered pages. Then paste the home page address into the inspection bar at the top and, if it says the page is not on Google, use the request indexing button.

3Read PerformanceFour numbers and one list.

Open Performance, then Search results. Look at the four tiles, then the Queries tab, where Haritt, Haritt Group and Harit Group are your brand searches. Data is kept for about sixteen months, so copy the numbers into a sheet once a month.

1
Read the four numbers together. For a name search you want a position near 1, and then a CTR that is fairly high, because the person was looking for you. In the made-up screen, the query "harit group" shows 8 impressions and no clicks. That is a person who half remembers the spelling and does not recognise your result. The fix is to name that spelling in the About page and the page description, which your schema already lists as an alternate name.
2
Impressions from many countries would also be a warning, because Haritt Group has no product for them. Use the Countries tab to see it. The Devices tab shows phone against desktop. Compare each number with your own last month, not with a benchmark.
3
One more useful habit is to repeat the plain name search yourself each month, in a private window, and write down where haritt.com appears. Your results can differ from other people's, so Search Console's average position is the better number, but the manual check shows you who the look-alikes are right now and whether a new one has appeared. Keep the notes next to the Search Console figures so you can compare them.
4
A made-up example: say after a month the Queries list shows "haritt" with 30 impressions and 6 clicks, and average position 5. That tells you a few people do search the name and a fifth of them click you. If instead the list is empty or shows only unrelated words, nobody is searching the name yet, and the answer is more word of mouth, not more search work.

Profiles, directories and keeping the story the same

The free tidy-up that lets search engines trust the name.

Same nameSame one-line descriptionSame founding yearLink to haritt.com
Open sheetAdd each linkRecheck each quarter

Search engines build confidence in a name when several independent places describe it the same way and link to the same site. The study found no such links to haritt.com. This is cheap to fix and needs no ads. The idea is to claim the places where a company is normally listed, write one fixed sentence about Haritt Group, and link it to haritt.com.

LLinkedIn and the social profilesAlready exist, make them match.

The LinkedIn company page exists. Your social handles are @haritt on YouTube and @harittgroup on Instagram, Facebook, X, Threads and GitHub. Check that each bio uses the fixed sentence and links to haritt.com. For links in bios, use the link maker on your admin page so the visits show up as a named source in analytics instead of Direct.

DDirectoriesChoose which listing belongs to which brand.

Crunchbase, Wellfound and similar company directories suit the Group. Product Hunt and AlternativeTo suit the HV Verse apps more, because they list products. Decide who owns each listing before creating it. Whether any of these exist is not on file, so mark each as done or open first.

YYour own venture sitesWhere links are allowed.

Haritt Developers may say it is part of Haritt Group, so it can link to haritt.com. HV Verse is branded separately, and only its footer line "A Haritt venture" may mention Haritt. Do not add the Group name anywhere else on HV Verse.

AA simple sheet to track itOne row per listing, checked every quarter.

Make a sheet with these columns: place, link to the profile, name used, description used, founding year used, link to haritt.com, date checked. Fill one row for every place on the list above. Once a quarter, open each link and compare the row with what the page says now. Directories sometimes change text or drop links, and the sheet is how you notice. It also shows at a glance which profiles are still missing.

Settle the founding year before you write any of these. Picking one line and using it everywhere removes the public conflict, and you can tell the longer story on the About page only if you want it.

Made-up example for a healthy site.

1
Because the Group has no sign-up and sells nothing, success here is modest and measurable: the name brings impressions, haritt.com sits near the top for it, and a fair share of those people click. If those three move in the right direction over a few months, the job is done and needs only a quarterly look.
2
When it fails, work down the list in the diagnosis above in order. First, is the page indexed? That is a yes or no answer from the inspection bar. Second, are people searching at all? That is the impressions number. Third, if impressions exist, where do you rank, and who sits above you? Open the search yourself and look. If a look-alike company is above you, the fix is more matching signals: profiles, links and a clear About page. Fourth, if you rank well but nobody clicks, the title and description are the problem, so rewrite them and wait a few weeks.
3
Do not be discouraged if the first weeks look empty. A new site with no outside links often gets few impressions, and brand searches only appear after real people have heard the name from somewhere. That is why the founders sharing one tagged link in personal chats, covered in the partners lesson, feeds this lesson: every person who hears the name and searches it shows up here as a brand impression. The two jobs support each other.
4
Change one thing at a time and write the date in the sheet, so you know what moved the numbers. Search results move slowly, so wait at least a few weeks before judging a change.
When to revisit: read Search Console monthly. If name impressions stay near zero for a few months, do not buy brand ads (see the last Group lesson). Put the effort into the people who will say the name for you: partners, clients and the founders' own network.

Two Search Console screens help with the first check. In URL inspection, the top line reads URL is on Google or URL is not on Google. If it is not, the Crawl block and the Indexing allowed? line usually say why. In the Page indexing report, the Indexed and Not indexed counts sit above a table called Why pages aren't indexed. A duplicate or a redirect in that table is often fine, while your home page in it is not.
