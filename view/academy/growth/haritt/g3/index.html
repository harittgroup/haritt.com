What partner and hiring conversations need from the Group page

The page is where a stranger checks you before replying.

Hears the nameOpens haritt.comChecks the workWrites or ignores
Hears of a roleOpens haritt.comLooks for who and whyApplies or ignores
1
There are two kinds of people who may want to talk to Haritt Group without ever buying anything: partners (people or small firms who could send you clients or users, or work beside you) and candidates (people who might one day work with you). Neither will find you through an ad. They hear the name from someone they trust, and then do the same thing every careful person does: they open haritt.com to see whether the company is real. So the Group page is part of the conversation even though you are not on it.
2
That changes what the page is for. It does not need a big call to action or a form. It needs to answer, in a few seconds, three questions for a stranger: who are you, what have you made, and how do I reach a person. Your files show that the page already sends people to the ventures and has a contact email that says replies come within two working days. What is missing is the work shown clearly (covered in the credibility lesson) and a short, plain description of who you would want to hear from.
3
Outbound, in marketing, means reaching out to people yourself instead of waiting to be found. At group level, it simply means a short list of partners and a few honest messages. No outbound or partnership activity is recorded in your files, no real sends have been made, and your rule is that nothing is sent without your OK. That rule stays.
Do you need this now? Not yet, but soon. Do the credibility and name-search work first, because a partner or candidate checking an empty page will not reply. Start the partner list while you fix the page, since writing it costs only an hour. Hiring: nothing on file shows a role open, so a hiring page is later, only when you actually want to hire.

Partner listNone written

Partners and candidates will judge you by the same things, so the readiness checks overlap. Go through them in order and stop at the first one you cannot pass.

1The page shows real workA visitor can see what exists.

Open haritt.com on a phone and ask whether someone who knows nothing about you could say, after one minute, what Haritt Group has built. If not, finish the work blocks first. A partner who cannot see work will not refer anyone, because they risk their own name when they recommend you.

Pass when: two ventures are shown with a screen, a date and a link each.

2The founding line is settledOne story.

A partner or candidate may check Justdial, LinkedIn and your About page. If they read 2026, 2020 and 2018, they will wonder what else does not match. Choose the line once.

Pass when: the three places match.

3People can reach you easilyPlain email, visible.

Contact is a mailto link to contact@haritt.com. A mailto link opens the visitor's email app, and on a laptop with no mail app set up, nothing happens. Show the address as visible text too, so it can be copied. You already promise a reply in two working days, so make sure you keep it.

Pass when: you can click the link on a phone and a laptop and also copy the address from the page.

4You know who you wantA short written list.

Write down who would be a good partner, in words, before names. For Haritt Developers, your own notes already suggest accountants, designers and printers who work with small businesses and could mention you to clients. For HV Verse, think of college groups or placement cells, but check the brand rule first, since HV Verse text never mentions Haritt.

Pass when: you have a list with a reason beside each entry.

5Your permission rule is clearNothing sent without your OK.

Your rule is that no real messages are sent without your approval. Keep a drafts folder, write each message there, and send only after you have read it again.

Pass when: you know where drafts live and who approves.

Part 3 of 5

Building the partner circle

A short list, one personal message each, tracked in a sheet.

1 List10 names and why each fits
2 DraftOne short personal message
3 SendAfter your OK, with a tagged link
4 TrackReplies in a sheet

The approach that fits a young group with no budget is small and personal. Do not mass email. Write ten names, each with one sentence on why they would care. Then write a different short message for each, because a template that sounds like a template is ignored.

TThe shortlistTen names with a reason each.

Start with people you already know or who already know a founder. Mix kinds: a couple who could refer Developers clients (an accountant, a designer, a printer who sees small businesses starting up), a couple of communities or groups where students gather for HV Verse, and a couple of peers who run similar small studios. For each row in your sheet write the name, how you know them, why they fit, what you would ask, and the date you contacted them.

TThe messageShort, specific, easy to say no to.

A good message has four parts: who you are in one line, what you noticed about them, one clear and small ask, and a link to haritt.com. For example, ask for ten minutes, or ask whether they would be open to mentioning you to a client who needs a website. Never ask for a big favour in the first message. Make the link tagged, using the link maker on your admin page, with the source set to email or whatsapp, so the visit shows up under that name in analytics.

TThe founder share linkThe easiest start of all.

Before any outreach, give the founders one tagged link and one sentence to use when they talk about the company in personal chats. Share it in five chats and note the replies. Then open the channel table in analytics and look for the source you set. This is the first real measure of how much word of mouth is working, and it costs nothing.

SSetting up the tracking for sharesWhere to click in analytics.

Use the link maker on your admin page to create one link per use: one for email, one for WhatsApp, one for the LinkedIn page. Each carries a source and a campaign name, which are small labels added to the end of the address so analytics can tell the visits apart. In GA4, open Reports, then Acquisition, then Traffic acquisition, and look for your source names. Menu names can change, so search for the closest words. Open every link yourself once after creating it, wait a day, and check that the visit appears under the label you chose.

KKeeping the circle warmA small thing that avoids a cold restart.

Once a partner replies, note what they said they care about and what they offered. When a venture ships something new, send a two-line update to the partners it may help, and nothing to the others. A circle of five who hear from you for a reason is worth more than fifty who hear from you every month. Never add anyone to a list without asking, since the privacy notice says there are no accounts or forms on the site.

Do not buy a sponsored directory slot. It needs approval, and without proof on the page it buys visits that do not convert.

Supporting hiring without ads

What to put on the page so a candidate understands you, if and when you hire.

WhoWhat the Group is

Two ventures, one parent, honest about size.

WhatThe work

Real products people can open.

HowHow people work here

Only what is true, such as AI use on the AI page.

ContactA route

One email, a reply promise.

No open role is on file, and haritt.com has no careers page. So hiring is a later step. But the habit to build now is cheap: make the Group page good enough that a candidate who finds it feels they understand the company. Do not post jobs on the page until you have a real one, because a stale or invented vacancy looks worse than none.

The Group site already has a page called How we use AI. For many candidates, especially students and freshers, that is useful, because it explains how the team works in a way they can judge. The Playbooks category on careers is written for job seekers, not for your own hiring, so do not treat it as a hiring page. The accurate way to use it is to let it show that the company writes clearly.

WWhen you do have a roleA short, plain page.

Write the role in plain words: what the person will do in the first month, which venture they will work on, whether it is paid or unpaid, the working hours and place, and how to apply. State only what is true. Use the same email as the contact route, and put a clear subject line in the instruction, so you can sort applications. Add a line saying how long you take to reply and keep to it. Track where each applicant heard of you with a tagged link, so you can see which sources bring real candidates.

TThe no-names ruleWhat it means for hiring.

Your rule is no personal names on public pages. A candidate cannot see who they would work for, which can feel risky. Offset it with specifics: the screens, the launch date, the way you answer email, and a named company role such as the person who will reply. If a candidate needs to know more, offer a short call rather than putting names on the page.

1
There are no ad numbers to read here, so you work from three things. First, the channel table in analytics: visits from your tagged sources, such as email, whatsapp or linkedin. Second, the Contact page and email click events already tracked. Third, the sheet where you note each reply, with the date and what happened. A count of replies matters more than visits, because a visit costs nothing.
2
A made-up example: say you send eight personal messages, and three people reply, and one agrees to mention you to a client. That is a clear, small win, and it tells you the message works. If eight go out and none reply, do not send more of the same. Check, in order, whether the page proves enough, whether you picked the right people, and whether the ask was small and clear. Fix one, test with the next small batch of five, and compare.
3
Also read the sheet for patterns by kind of person. If the accountants reply and the printers do not, spend your next batch on accountants and drop the others for now. If personal WhatsApp messages get answered and emails do not, switch the channel. Patterns need at least a handful of rows before you trust them, so do not decide anything from two messages.
4
For hiring, the useful number is the share of applicants you would actually talk to. If you get applications but few are suitable, the role text or the place you shared it is wrong. If you get none, check that the contact route works by sending yourself a test application from a phone.
5
Stop sending to any one person after one follow-up. Partners who do not reply twice are not hostile; they are busy, and a third message costs you goodwill.
When to revisit: once the Group page shows real work and the founding line is fixed, start the shortlist. Add a hiring page only when you have a role. Review the sheet every month.
