Where to go from here

Now, next and later

The order to do things in. Small-budget options are suggestions only: nothing is spent, launched, sent or changed on a live site without your approval.

Haritt Group: next steps

Haritt Group has no product to sell, so growth here means three things: being found, being believed, and sending people to the right venture. These six approaches fit that, starting with the cheapest.

Strong today

On-page SEO for the brand name, GA4 plus the own counter, a link maker, and an honest privacy notice.

Building

Social accounts and a draft plan, a set of tracked events, and cross-links to the two ventures.

Missing

A written goal, proof, lead capture, Search Console data, profiles and backlinks, and any test.

Now

Fix the foundation

  • Write the one-sentence job of haritt.com (card 4)
  • Check Search Console and the Returning bug (card 11)
  • Choose one founding line (card 26)
  • Tag every bio and signature link (card 30)
Next

Build proof and reach

  • Claim profiles with one fixed sentence (card 17)
  • Publish the Playbooks drafts (card 13)
  • Add a home section that shows both ventures first (card 23)
  • Show the contact email as text, or add a form (card 24)
Later

Test and learn

  • Run the five-person message test (card 38)
  • Start a learning log (card 35)
  • Review four KPIs every week (card 32)
  • Decide on a brand-name ad test (card 18)
Haritt Developers: next steps

Haritt Developers sells custom projects to businesses. Buyers are few and decisions take long, so the approaches below favour trust, proof and referral over reach. Paid comes last.

Strong today

A clear 7-service offer and process, a working enquiry form with an admin inbox, a rich set of events and a dashboard, and a strong brand-name search position on 14 Oct.

Building

Google Business Profile and Justdial assets are prepared but unverified. Social and intro-email plans are in draft. GA4 is added but not verified, and the privacy text needs aligning.

Missing

An ideal client, client proof, a source on each lead, a pipeline after the enquiry (no qualified, proposal or won stage), enquiry counts, project value, and any cost or CAC record.

Now

Fix the foundation

  • Capture the source of every enquiry, plus a self-reported answer (card 30)
  • Use the same founding-year line everywhere (card 12)
  • Check that GA4 receives enquiry_sent (card 29)
  • Choose or confirm the ideal client (card 7)
Next

Build proof and pipeline

  • Verify the Google profile and publish the prepared posts (card 12)
  • Write the first case study, with approval (card 26)
  • Add pipeline stages and project value to the inbox (card 47)
  • Add a first-reply template and a reply rule (card 42)
Later

Grow what works

  • Ask for referrals and give partners tagged links (card 45)
  • Write question pages for searches that do not use the brand name (card 11)
  • Create a package or a "from" price for the top service (card 46)
  • Run a capped paid test, if you approve it (card 21)
