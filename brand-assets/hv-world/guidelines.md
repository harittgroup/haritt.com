# HV World brand guidelines

Role: Digital product ecosystem. Website: https://hvworld.haritt.com.

The product ecosystem: HV Reset, HV Tests, HV Vault and HV AI. Clean, light, Apple-like surfaces with a deep blue accent and one colour per product.

Theme: Light only. The HV World site does not ship a dark theme (individual apps may differ).

## Logos
- Primary logo / app icon: https://haritt.com/icons/hv-world.svg (SVG). Orbit mark on a blue-violet gradient tile.
- HV Reset icon: https://haritt.com/icons/hv-reset.svg (SVG). Product icon.
- HV Vault icon: https://haritt.com/icons/hv-vault.png (PNG). Product icon.
- HV Test icon: https://haritt.com/icons/hv-test.png (PNG). Product icon.
- HV AI icon: https://haritt.com/icons/hv-ai.svg (SVG). Product icon.

Not available: Wordmark logo ("HV World" text lockup); Monochrome logo; Dark-background version.

## Usage
- Use the logo files as supplied. Never stretch, squash, rotate, recolour or add effects.
- Minimum size: 24px on screen for circular badges and app icons; 32px or larger when the text inside must be readable.
- Clear space: keep at least 25% of the logo's width empty on every side.
- Circular badges and app icons work on light and dark backgrounds; avoid busy photos behind them.
- Keep each brand in its own colours and fonts. Do not mix tokens across brands.
- Cross-brand links: show the venture's own logo and name, and link to its own website.
- Headers: logo plus brand name; a parent link ("by Haritt Group") may sit beside a venture's name.
- Naming: brand-assets/<brand>/logos/<brand>-<variant>.<ext>; CSS variables use the brand prefix (--hg-, --hd-, --hvw-).

## Fonts
- HVSora (Sora): Display headings, weights 400, 600–700. Self-hosted: /media/fonts/Sora-Regular.ttf, Sora-SemiBold.ttf · SIL Open Font License 1.1
- System UI stack: Body, UI, weights system. Device fonts, nothing to license

Tokens: colors.css, typography.css, tokens.json in this folder. They document the live site; changing them does not change the website.
