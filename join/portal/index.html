Haritt Joining Portal

Where it lives

  • join.haritt.com for joiners (personal invite link, 7-day expiry).
  • Founders manage it inside haritt.com/admin (new "Team onboarding" page, same login).

Joiner flow

  • Invite link > email code or Google > Joining Pack (each section opened + agree) > Details form > Agreement + NDA e-sign > My journey.
  • Journey: stages with tasks (tick, upload, meeting), weekly 3-line update, signed documents.

Agreement e-sign

  • Same engine as developers.haritt.com/sign, copied and restyled to Haritt Group.
  • Joiner signs first, then both founders. Signed PDF + audit record (time, email, device, fingerprint) emailed to everyone. English + Hindi.

Founders can edit everything

  • Joining Pack sections, agreement templates (with auto-fill fields, versioned), details form fields, email texts.
  • Stage templates (default: Day 0 / 1–7 / 7 / 8–30 / 30). Per joiner: add, remove, reorder stages and tasks; set role and level.

Tech (free)

  • Static pages on haritt.com + existing Firebase (data) + one new Apps Script on the Group account (emails via Resend, PDFs in Drive).
  • One Apps Script paste at the end, plus one DNS record for join.haritt.com.

Build order after approval

  1. Admin: templates, Pack, stages, invite.
  2. Joiner portal: verify, Pack, form, sign, journey.
  3. E-sign + emails, end-to-end test with a test joiner, then live.

Joining Agreement and Non-Disclosure

Haritt Group · Ref HG-JN-2611-A7K2 · 19 Nov 2026

This agreement is between Haritt Group ("the Company") and Aman Sharma, Jaipur ("the Member"), joining in the role of Client Growth at the level of Core Member.

1. Nature of association. There is no fixed time commitment and no lock-in. The Member commits to the four expectations: communication, professionalism, ownership and confidentiality.

2. Confidential information. All client details, plans, numbers, revenue, tools, logins and internal discussions are confidential. The Member will not share them with anyone, including friends, family or on social media, during or after the association.

3. Access. Access is given step by step, only as the role needs, and is removed the same day the association ends.

4. Levels and ownership. Levels are earned by behaviour. No equity or ownership is implied by this agreement; any such matter is decided separately and only in writing.

5. Ending. Either side may end the association with notice by email. Clause 2 survives the end of the association.
