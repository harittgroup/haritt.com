2Are you sure you want this?

Say yes only after thinking honestly. This is what joining Haritt really means.

This is the kind of company we are building, and you need to own it.

  • You are joining an early-stage company, not a finished one. Things change fast, and you help build them.
  • You are expected to think like an owner. Nobody will chase you for work.
  • There is no salary or fixed payment right now. Any payment is agreed separately, in writing.
  • No fixed hours. We look at attitude, honesty and results, not time spent.
  • What you put in decides what you become here. Growth is earned, never given by default.
If this excites you, read on. If not, that is completely fine. Just tell us honestly.

3Why everything is written here

We have learned from experience. These things have happened before, and we don't want them again:

People said "yes, I'll do it" on call, then did nothing.
People went silent for days or weeks without telling anyone.
People left without informing, leaving work half done.
Internal things were shared outside "casually", with friends.
Promises were made to clients that the team could not keep.
Later: "nobody told me", "it was not written", "I didn't read it".

So we wrote everything down, in simple words. Please read every page yourself before signing. If something is unclear, ask first. After signing, "I didn't know" will not be accepted.

4What we expect: 4 commitments

We don't ask for hours. We ask for these four things.

CommunicationReply within 24 hours. Say problems early.
"I can't finish this by Friday, I'll send it Sunday." Said on Wednesday.
No reply for 3 days, then "sorry, I was busy".
ProfessionalismDo what you say. Respect people and dates.
Joins the call on time, prepared, and follows up the same day.
Tells a client a price or date without asking the founding team.
OwnershipTreat Haritt as your own company.
Sees a mistake on the website and reports it with a fix idea.
"That's not my work, someone else will see it."
ConfidentialityEverything inside stays inside.
A friend asks "how much does Haritt earn?" You say "that's internal."
Shows a team chat screenshot to "just one friend".

5Your role and what you can become

Your role: Client Growth, finding and talking to businesses and bringing good clients. Other roles are Marketing & Content and Operations. Levels are earned by behaviour, not by time.

LEVEL 1Core MemberReliable, follows the rules, communicates on time.Everyone starts here.
LEVEL 2Founding TeamOwns an area, brings ideas, others rely on you.Example: runs client outreach without reminders.
LEVEL 3Co-founderCarries responsibility like a founder.Decided by the founding team together, after proven behaviour.

Levels can go up or down. A level or title does not mean shares or ownership. That is only ever decided separately, in writing.

8After Day 10

If approved, you become an official member of Haritt Group.Your role and level are confirmed, you keep your company email and access, and we sign the full Team Agreement together. If not approved, we part ways politely, and confidentiality still applies.

9Simple rules for Step 1

Use company email and accounts for company work, once given.
No promises, prices or dates to clients without the founding team.
Honest work only. No fake numbers, clients, reviews or copied work.
Don't call yourself a founder, owner or partner. Use only your given role.
Never share your login. Access is step by step, only what your role needs.
What you create for Haritt belongs to Haritt.

10Confidentiality

This is the most important rule. Everything inside Haritt is private: clients, prices, money, plans, ideas, designs, code, tools, logins, internal chats and decisions. Don't share it with anyone outside the team, including friends, family or online. No screenshots or copies for personal use.

Example: You hear about a new client in a team call. Telling a friend "we just got a big jewellery brand" is a breach, even without the name.

This starts today and continues forever, even if you leave or don't sign the full agreement.

11Money, ownership and leaving

  • No payment unless the founding team agrees in writing before the work starts. No shares or equity from this document or anything said on a call.
  • You can leave any time. Just tell the founding team. Leaving politely is always fine. Going silent is not. No reply for 3 days in Step 1, without telling us, means you have left.
  • Before leaving, hand over your work and delete company information from your devices. Access is removed the same day.
  • Haritt can end this at once for a confidentiality breach, dishonest work or misuse of access.

12Ownership: act like the owner

We are building this company with people who think like founders. The higher your role and level, the more you own. If you join to take an ownership role, like a co-founder, this is what it means:

If you own it, you own everything.

  • You act as the owner of the company, not as someone helping from outside.
  • You act as the brand. How you talk, work and behave is how people see Haritt.
  • If the brand or your area gets into trouble, it is your responsibility to handle it. No excuses, no blaming others.
  • You don't wait to be told. You see what needs doing and you do it, end to end.
The full details come in the full Team Agreement. We are telling you now, so it is clear from day one.
