1Who we are

This agreement is between Haritt Group ("Haritt", "we", "the Company") and the person joining the team ("you", "the Member").

Haritt Group runs two ventures. This agreement covers both, and any new venture we start later.

Haritt GroupThe parent company of both ventures.
Haritt DevelopersBusiness solutions company. Helps businesses solve real problems, end to end.
HV VerseProduct venture. Builds apps for students and individuals.

Address: Behind Shiva Talkies, Sahyog Nagar, Bharatpur, Rajasthan 321001, India.

2Why this agreement

We discussed everything on call before you joined. This agreement puts it in writing, so both sides have the same clear understanding and nothing is left to memory.

3How we work together

  • You join as a team member. This is not employment, partnership or ownership.
  • No fixed hours and no lock-in. We don't count hours. We look at attitude and results.
  • No payment by default. Any pay, incentive or commission applies only if a Founder agrees to it in writing (email is enough) before the work starts.
  • You are free to study, work or do other things, as long as you follow sections 8 and 11.

4What we expect: 4 commitments

Instead of hours, you commit to these four things. They are the heart of this agreement.

CommunicationReply within 24 hours. Stuck or running late? Say it early. Send a weekly 3-line update: done, doing next, blocked.
ProfessionalismDo what you say. Respect clients, team and deadlines. Be polite and honest everywhere.
OwnershipTreat Haritt as your own company. See a problem? Fix it or raise it. Finish what you start.
ConfidentialityEverything inside stays inside. Not with friends, family or online. See section 8.
If you can't continue, just tell us. Leaving politely is always fine. Going silent is not. No reply for 14 days without telling us may be treated as leaving.

6How joining works

Joining happens in stages. The next stage opens after the Founders review the current one.

7Working rules

  • Use only your company email and company accounts for company work.
  • No promises, prices, discounts or deadlines to any client without a Founder's approval.
  • Honest work only. No fake numbers, clients, reviews or testimonials. No copied work.
  • Don't talk about clients, projects or internal matters in public or on social media without approval.
  • Don't call yourself a Founder, owner, partner or director. Use only the role and level given to you.

8Confidentiality (NDA)

This is the most important section. You keep all company information private, while you are with us and after you leave.

Private information includes: client names, contacts and requirements; prices, quotes and contracts; revenue, payments and any money matters; plans, ideas and launch dates; designs, code, documents and processes; tools, logins and passwords; internal chats, calls and decisions; and any problem inside the company.

You will not:

  • Share it with anyone outside the team: friends, family, classmates, colleagues or social media. "Just telling one friend" is also a breach.
  • Take screenshots, recordings or copies for personal use.
  • Use it for yourself or to help anyone compete with Haritt.

You will: use it only for company work, keep it on company accounts, and tell a Founder at once if anything is shared by mistake.

Need to know. You get only the information your role needs. This is how we protect everyone, not a sign of distrust.

Not covered: information that is already public, that you knew before joining (shown in writing), or that the law requires you to share (tell us first, where allowed).

How long: starts the day you receive any information. Client data, money matters, logins and trade secrets stay private forever. Everything else stays private for 3 years after you leave.

9Access and accounts

  • Access is given step by step, only what your role needs. It can be reduced or removed at any time.
  • Never given to team members: finance, billing, admin panel, payment accounts and brand social media logins (unless both Founders approve in writing).
  • Company accounts belong to Haritt. Don't share your login. Use strong passwords and 2-step verification.
  • When you leave, all access is removed the same day.

10Who owns the work

  • Everything you create for Haritt (designs, code, content, ideas, data, client relationships) belongs to Haritt from the moment it is made.
  • You can mention your Haritt role in your CV or profile. You can show specific work in a portfolio only with written approval.
  • What you made before joining, or make outside Haritt without using our information, stays yours.

11Clients and other work

  • Tell the Founders in advance about any work similar to Haritt's, so we can talk about it openly.
  • Any client or lead that comes to you because of your Haritt role belongs to Haritt.
  • While with us and for 12 months after, you won't use our information to take our clients, leads or partners, or pull team members away.

12Equity and ownership

Nothing in this agreement, or said on any call or chat, gives you shares, equity, partnership or profit share. Any such matter is discussed separately and is valid only in a separate document signed by both Founders and you. It is never assumed.

13Leaving

  • Either side can end this at any time by email. Please give a few days' notice and a proper handover.
  • Haritt can end it immediately for a confidentiality breach, dishonest work, misuse of access, or harm to clients or Haritt's name.
  • When you leave: hand over your work, delete company information from your devices and confirm by email, stop using Haritt's name and title, and don't speak badly about Haritt, its team or clients in public.
  • Sections 8, 10, 11, 12 and 13 continue after you leave.

14If rules are broken

A breach, especially of confidentiality, can seriously harm Haritt and its clients. Haritt may remove you at once, remove all access, ask for all information back, and take action under Indian law, including court orders and damages.

15Your personal details

We use your details from the Joining Form only for onboarding, communication and records. We keep them private and don't share them outside Haritt, unless the law requires it.

16Legal basics

  • This agreement and the Joining Pack are the full understanding between us. They replace earlier verbal talks on joining.
  • Changes are valid only in writing. We may update the Joining Pack and will share the new version with you.
  • This agreement can be signed online. Electronic signatures are valid under the Information Technology Act, 2000. Everyone gets the signed copy by email.
  • Notices are sent by email.
  • If one part is found invalid, the rest still applies.
  • Indian law applies. Courts in Bharatpur, Rajasthan. We will always try to solve things by talking first.
