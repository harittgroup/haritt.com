01 / CHAPTER

The central recommendation

Keep the architecture understandable: Haritt Group is the parent brand, Haritt Development is the services business, and HV World is the product family. Explain these relationships consistently without creating extra brands, domains or accounts without a clear reason.

02 / CHAPTER

Brand architecture

Use Haritt Group for company-level identity and trust. Use Haritt Development when speaking to service clients about digital delivery. Use HV World for the owned product ecosystem. Make the parent/product relationship clear on relevant pages and profiles.

03 / CHAPTER

Website and domain plan

Use the existing company website as the central source of truth. Give company information, services, products and contact details clear destinations. Avoid buying new domains at launch unless a real user journey or business need justifies it.

04 / CHAPTER

Audience and positioning

For clients, state who you help, what you deliver, what proof exists and how to enquire. For product users, explain the problem solved, demonstrate the real workflow, state beta limitations honestly and make feedback easy.

05 / CHAPTER

LinkedIn and social profiles

Prioritise a complete founder profile and a consistent official company page. Create separate product accounts only when the audience is distinct and a sustainable publishing plan exists. Keep names, descriptions, links and visuals consistent.

06 / CHAPTER

Content operating system

Publish useful insights, genuine build-in-public updates, real product demonstrations, user lessons and founder perspectives. Every item should serve a defined audience, deliver a takeaway and have one clear call to action.

07 / CHAPTER

Search and measurement

Use descriptive titles, accurate descriptions, canonical URLs, a valid sitemap and Search Console. Track qualified enquiries, product activation, repeat use and useful feedback. Treat reach as a diagnostic metric, not the final business outcome.

08 / CHAPTER

90-day roadmap

Days 1–30: establish and verify the foundation. Days 31–60: publish consistently and speak to target audiences. Days 61–90: focus on the messages and channels that create qualified enquiries, useful feedback or real product use.

09 / CHAPTER

Execution checklist

Verify all claims and product status; test mobile layouts, links and contact routes; align profiles and logos; configure analytics intentionally; interview users; review results weekly; document decisions and owners.

Working note: Verify current product status, website behaviour and platform guidance before implementing changes.
