HV World AI Discoverability Guide

A practical guide to defining the brand accurately, publishing authoritative information, helping search systems discover it and keeping AI answers consistent.

01 / CHAPTER

Understand discoverability

Treat discoverability as four connected jobs: define verified brand facts, publish them on reliable pages, make those pages technically discoverable, and maintain the facts as products change. Publication never guarantees indexing or inclusion in AI answers.

02 / CHAPTER

Create one source of truth

Maintain an approved brand reference and public website copy with the same facts. Record the official name, purpose, product names, current availability, canonical links, contact routes and review date. Mark unknowns rather than guessing.

03 / CHAPTER

Structure the website

Give the homepage, About, Products and FAQ pages distinct jobs. Use clear titles, descriptive headings, internal links and accurate metadata. Explain each product in plain language and clearly state its current status.

04 / CHAPTER

Check search access

Verify the site in Search Console, inspect important URLs, check canonical URLs and ensure public pages are not unintentionally marked noindex. Keep the sitemap valid and submitted. Crawling is possible, but rankings are not guaranteed.

05 / CHAPTER

Understand crawler choices

Review current publisher guidance before changing robots rules. Search discovery crawlers and model-training crawlers can have different purposes and should be treated as separate decisions. Verify any crawler rules against current official documentation.

06 / CHAPTER

Use structured data carefully

Organisation and product structured data should reflect visible, accurate page content. Use canonical URLs and official logo assets. Do not invent ratings, prices, features, legal details or endorsements.

07 / CHAPTER

Maintain an AI reference

Keep a dedicated project or workspace with current official facts and links. Instruct it to prefer verified facts, distinguish live features from plans, and flag uncertainty. Update the source document first, then refresh working references.

08 / CHAPTER

Publish credible references

Maintain official profiles and useful product documentation. Seek genuine independent coverage where appropriate. Never fabricate press mentions, testimonials, citations or third-party endorsements.

09 / CHAPTER

Test results

Record important URLs in Search Console, review crawler access and test relevant brand queries in search and AI systems. If answers are wrong, find missing or contradictory sources and correct them. Keep a dated record of the query and result.

10 / CHAPTER

Rollout plan

Days 1–2: approve brand facts and the master guide. Days 3–5: publish and review core pages. Days 6–7: check indexing, robots rules and sitemap. Week 2: maintain the AI reference and run baseline tests. Review after meaningful changes.

11 / CHAPTER

Implementation checklist

Verify the official definition, product names and live status; publish core pages; check titles and canonical URLs; validate sitemap and robots rules; review structured data; record baseline queries; assign an owner and review date.

Working note: Verify current product status, website behaviour and platform guidance before implementing changes.
