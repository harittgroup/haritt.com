01 / CHAPTER

Executive recommendation

Start with the simplest structure customers can understand. Haritt Group is the parent identity, Haritt Development is the client-services business, and HV World is the product umbrella when it accurately describes the products.

02 / CHAPTER

Separate the decisions

Legal structure, brand structure, websites and social profiles are related but do not have to map one-to-one. Decide each separately. Not every product needs its own company, domain or social page.

03 / CHAPTER

One-domain approach

Use the existing company domain as the central reference. Make company, services, products, about and contact pages easy to find. Add separate product destinations when they answer distinct user questions or support a clear journey.

04 / CHAPTER

Social account architecture

Keep the founder profile credible and useful, and maintain the official company page. Create dedicated product profiles only when a distinct audience and regular publishing capacity justify them.

05 / CHAPTER

Positioning and messaging

For service prospects, explain who you help, what you deliver and how to enquire. For product users, describe the use case, demonstrate the current product and clearly label beta status. Avoid unsupported superlatives.

06 / CHAPTER

Content and distribution

Establish a sustainable rhythm before expanding channels. Repurpose useful insights into LinkedIn posts, short videos, product notes and website articles. Use a call to action that fits the content.

07 / CHAPTER

Analytics and technical setup

Verify the website in Search Console. Check indexing, page titles, descriptions, mobile usability and working links. Add analytics only when it answers a defined question and matches actual privacy practices.

08 / CHAPTER

90-day execution

Month 1: establish brand and website basics. Month 2: publish and interview target audiences. Month 3: assess what drove qualified enquiries, useful feedback and real product usage, then keep, change or stop activities based on evidence.

09 / CHAPTER

Decision gates

Do not buy domains or create accounts just because they are available. Do not scale paid promotion before the offer, landing page and follow-up process work. Invest further when evidence supports the next step.

Working note: Verify current product status, website behaviour and platform guidance before implementing changes.
