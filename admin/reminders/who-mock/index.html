Who gets which reminder

The simple idea: a reminder never goes to a named person. It goes to a role. You pick the role once when you add someone. When their role changes, their mails change by themselves.

Owner always gets danger mailsExpiry, money, security and problems: Owner, whatever else is set.
New role starts freshA person promoted today gets mails from today. They do not receive old ones.

5One reminder, one exception (how it works with 2 or 3 people in a role)

Example: Unsigned agreements goes to Owner and Co-founders by role. Tap a person to turn that reminder off or on for them. Everyone is a @haritt.com ID.

Who can change thisOnly the Owner (you). A co-founder cannot switch another co-founder off, so nobody can block anybody.
What a person can do aloneMute their own normal reminders (chat, meetings). They cannot mute danger mails: expiry, money, security, problems.
