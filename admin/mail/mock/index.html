Haritt Group · Mail

One Mail Center

Every mail Haritt sends or receives, in one place. One list, one sender rule, one log.

Mock only. Numbers in Activity are examples, not real. The list in "All mails" is the real list of mails the system sends today.

The problem today

Found by reading all scripts.

MESSYToday
  • 10 files send mail: joining script, e-sign script, Workspace script, enquiry script, backup script, the joining worker, 2 GitHub jobs, and 2 HV Verse scripts.
  • 20+ sender addresses (developers@, verify@, docs@, join@, hr@, team@, hello@, support@, alerts@ and people's own).
  • 30+ different mails, each with its own wording and design.
  • 5 admin pages touch mail: Send email, Marketing, Email addresses, Reminders, System status.
  • No single place to see what went out, who got it, and what failed.
SIMPLEAfter
  • One list of every mail type: when it goes, from whom, to whom, on or off.
  • One sending rule: each kind of mail has exactly one sender.
  • One log: every mail from every script, tagged and searchable.
  • One page: Mail Center in the admin, replacing the five.
  • One alert: you get told when mails fail or bounce.

Steps (no big risk, one by one)

1

Map and tag

Make the single mail list (a file in the repo). Add a tag to every mail the scripts send. Nothing changes for the people receiving mails.

2

Mail Center page

New admin page: Activity log, All mails (with on/off, reading the same switches as Reminders), Senders. Old pages stay until you are happy.

3

Clean senders

Move every mail to its one sender (see Senders tab). Personal Gmail IDs never shown. Same look for every mail within a brand.

4

Merge pages and alerts

Send email, Marketing and Email addresses become tabs inside Mail Center. Failed or bounced mails raise one alert. Weekly summary to you.

  • Say "bana do" and I start with step 1 and 2.
