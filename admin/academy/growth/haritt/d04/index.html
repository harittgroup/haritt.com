Local search for a business with no office

Two free tools: your Google profile, and Search Console to see how you appear.

Shows when someone searches your name or your service.

vs
Search results for your siteYour pages on Google

1
SEO (search engine optimisation) is the work of being found on Google without paying. Local search is the part where Google shows businesses for a place or for a service near the searcher. A Google Business Profile is the free listing that appears on Google Search and Maps with your name, photos, website link and reviews. Google Search Console is a second free tool, and it does the opposite job: it tells you how your website appears in Google, which searches showed it and how many people clicked.
2
Haritt Developers has no office, so it is what Google calls a service-area business: you visit customers in person, instead of customers walking in. For this type you hide the street address and list the areas you serve instead. That is exactly how your prepared profile is set up, as a software company with a service area and no office address. Which areas you serve is not on file. Justdial says Bharatpur, but you have not confirmed that as your service area, so decide it with your co-founder.
3
Be realistic about what this does. An owner looking for a web developer may type the service, a city, or the name of a studio a friend mentioned. The profile helps most with that last case: when someone searches "Haritt Developers", a clear panel with your photos, website and reviews builds trust. On 14 Oct your name already ranked #1 and #2, so the profile adds to something that works. Whether people search for your services without your name is unknown, and Search Console is how you find out.
4
Do you need this now? Search Console: yes. The profile: yes, but only if it is honest for you, because Google says a profile is for businesses that meet customers face to face, and not for online-only ones. Both tools are free and take an afternoon, and the assets are already prepared. The profile is also the cheapest way to get your first public reviews. What to focus on instead if you cannot get the profile verified yet: fix the Justdial details and set up Search Console, which does not depend on a phone check.

The profile is prepared with logos at two sizes, a cover image in two versions, 10 photos, five weekly Update posts with the button "Learn more", and two videos (services and our work). The site has keyword titles, schema (hidden code that tells search engines what the site is), a 10-question FAQ, a sitemap, robots and llms.txt. The brand name ranks.

BWhat does not matchFacts that disagree across the web.

Justdial says "Established 2018, Bharatpur", while the About page says "Founded in 2026". The X handle is @harittdeveloper while the others are @harittdevelopers. The developers@ address is described two different ways. Google compares your details across sites, and people compare them too, so choose one set of facts first: name, founding line, website, email, phone and service areas. Write them in one sheet and copy from it everywhere.

CThe account problemA note says the developers@ account was blocked.

On 12 Oct the Google account for developers@ was blocked by a phone-verification limit. Decide which Google account will own the profile. Prefer one the company controls, not a personal account, so the profile survives if one person leaves. You can add more owners and managers later, but you need one working account today.

DReadiness checkFour things before you click.

Do you have one signed-in Google account that can receive a verification code? Have you chosen the founding line and the service areas? Do you have a way to answer a phone number or message you list? Is Search Console status known? If not, do those four things first. Creating a profile with wrong details, or with a made-up address, can lead to a suspension, and getting it back is slow.

Check eligibility first. Google's help page says Business Profiles are for businesses that meet customers in person during business hours, including service providers who visit customers. It says online-only businesses are not eligible. If every Haritt Developers project is done remotely and you never meet a client, a profile may be refused or later suspended. My reading: only create it if you do visit local clients. If not, skip the profile and put your effort into Search Console and the Justdial fix.

Menu names change often, so follow the idea if a label looks different. Sign in to the Google account that will own the profile. Go to business.google.com and choose to add your business, or search for your business name and use the "Add your business to Google" option.

1Name and categoryReal name only. Pick the closest category.

Type Haritt Developers exactly as it appears on your site. Do not add keywords such as "best web developer". Choose the primary category that matches what you do, which in your files is Software company. If Google offers extra categories, add one or two that truly match your services.

2Location questionSay no to a customer-visit location.

When Google asks whether you have a location customers can visit, answer no, then add your service areas. Google may still ask for a private address during setup to send a code. Use only a place where you genuinely work, and tell Google to hide it. Never use a made-up address or a virtual mailbox. If you have no honest address to give, say so to your co-founder and look at what verification options Google offers.

3Contact detailsPhone is optional, a wrong one is not.

A phone number is not on file for Haritt Developers, and the form does not ask for one. Add one only if someone will answer it. Add the website, and tag the link, for example by ending it with ?utm_source=google&utm_medium=organic&utm_campaign=gbp, so that visits from the profile can be told apart in your counter. Your counter may file that under the campaign table rather than a named channel.

4VerifyChoose a method Google offers.

Google decides which methods are offered, and they can include a live video call or video recording, a phone or text code, an email, or a postcard. Service-area businesses are often asked for a short video that shows you run the business, as Google instructs on screen. Follow Google's on-screen instructions and do not tick a method you cannot complete. Verification can take days, so start early.

5Fill in the restAfter verification, complete every section.

In Edit profile, open Business information to set hours (if useful), service areas and description, and open Services to list the seven services. Then add the prepared photos, logos and cover. A complete profile looks credible and gives Google more to match against searches.

Your notes disagree on whether Search Console is set up: some say you need to do it, the admin pages say it is set up, and no data exists in any file. So start by checking. Go to search.google.com/search-console, sign in with the Google account that should own it, and open the property selector at the top left. If you see haritt.com or developers.haritt.com, it exists, and you should confirm you are an owner. If not, add it.

1Add the propertyDomain or URL prefix.

Choose Add property. A Domain property covers the whole haritt.com domain including developers.haritt.com, and is verified by adding a TXT record in your DNS (Cloudflare, per your files). A URL prefix property covers one address and offers easier methods such as an HTML tag. Domain is the better long-term choice, since one property then shows all your Haritt sites.

2Submit the sitemapSo Google knows your pages.

In the left menu open Indexing, then Sitemaps, type sitemap.xml and submit. The status should say Success. Then use the search bar at the top for URL inspection, paste an important page, and if it is not on Google choose Request indexing.

3Read PerformanceFour numbers, two tabs.

Open Performance, then Search results. The report has four metrics. The tab row below the chart groups the same numbers by Queries, Pages, Countries, Devices, Search appearance and Dates. Data arrives with a delay of a day or two, and a new property needs days before anything shows.

TThe profile numbers, one by oneFrom the Performance page.

Views count unique people who saw the profile, once a day each, so the number is lower than raw page loads. Direction requests will be near zero for you, which is normal. Searches show the terms. If every term holds your name, nobody finds you by service yet.

When local search disappoints, the cause is usually in one of these places, and each leaves different marks. Check from the cheapest to the hardest.

1Is it set up?Verified, correct, complete.

Open the profile while signed in and read any warnings. A suspended or unverified profile shows nothing, however good the content. Also confirm the name, area and website match your fact sheet.

2Is Google seeing the site?Indexing and sitemap.

In Search Console, open Indexing then Pages to see how many pages are on Google and the reasons for pages that are not. Use URL inspection on the services page. If a page is not indexed, fix the reason shown and request indexing again.

3Is the search real?Do people look for this?

If your queries are only your own name, the demand for your services without your name may be small, or you have no page that answers it. The fix is a page for one real question from your enquiries, not more posting on the profile.

4Is the page converting?Visit to form.

If tagged visits arrive and nobody starts the form, return to the first lesson: look at the funnel, the proof and the form length.

Change one thing at a time, wait for a fresh month of data, and compare against the previous month. Write down what you believed, what you changed and what moved. Revisit the profile after your first reviews and after each finished project, and check Search Console once a month. If after several months of correct setup neither tool produces any enquiry, local search is not your best channel, so go back to the channel scoring in lesson d03 and test the next candidate.
