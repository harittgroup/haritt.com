Where HV Verse stands today

HV Verse is a set of free apps. The journey below starts with someone who has never heard of it and ends with a user who invites a friend.

HV Verse

HV Verse is a set of free apps for consumers, and it has no way of earning money yet. Growth here means people who try an app, get value, come back and tell others. Almost nothing is measured as a number yet, and this page says so plainly.

Where to go from here

Now, next and later

The order to do things in. Small-budget options are suggestions only: nothing is spent, launched, sent or changed on a live site without your approval.

HV Verse: next steps

HV Verse has no revenue, so the aim is people who use an app, come back and recommend it. These six approaches suit a small team with no ad budget. Pick one to lead, and let the rest support it.

Strong today

Each app is free and does one clear job. You have a first-party counter with privacy protections, UTM tagging rules and short links. The accounts live under one name. Staying organic-only is a firm decision.

Building

Activation events exist, but there is no agreed definition. A retention view exists, but it shows zeros. Student Beta assets exist, but they are not ready. Welcome emails exist, but they have no unsubscribe link.

Missing

Real user numbers, a chosen first group of users, a North Star (the one number that shows value), a source on Beta applications, a measured referral loop, search data, and a clean rename.

Now

Fix the foundation

  • Agree the activation definition for each app (card 42)
  • Fix the Returning / active-users zeros (card 33)
  • Clean up the Student Beta posters and add a source field (card 16)
  • Match the privacy text to GA4 (card 34)
Next

Measure the loops

  • Tag scorecard shares and QR codes (card 45)
  • Send a first Beta batch after your go-ahead (card 44)
  • Point the short links to the profiles (card 14)
  • Add Search Console for thehvverse.com (card 11)
Later

Grow what works

  • Send a nudge to quiet HV Reset users (card 43)
  • Add long-tail pages for the apps (card 13)
  • Run an institute pilot with a clear ask (card 48)
  • Decide the Android plan for HV Reset (card 46)
