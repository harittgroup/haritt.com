What can go wrong

The challenges

Every one has a fix. We list them so nothing surprises the team later. Red needs the most care.

1

Reminders when the app is closedNeeds care

Problem: a website can only remind you while it is open. Reset is all about reminders.

Fix: inside the app we use the phone's own alarm and notification service. Some brands (Xiaomi, Oppo, Vivo) shut apps down to save battery, so we test on more than one phone.

2

Sign in with GoogleNeeds care

Problem: Google does not allow its sign-in inside an app that is really a website in a box.

Fix: we use the phone's own Google sign-in. One wrong setting breaks it, so we test it early.

3

People's data and privacyNeeds care

Problem: Reset saves plans, task times, focus ratings and AI chats on our server, tied to a Google account. Both stores want to know exactly what we keep and why.

Fix: a clear privacy policy page, honest answers in the stores' data forms, and a delete-account button inside the app that removes all of a person's data. Reset has no such button yet, so we add it. Users under 18 need extra care because HV World is open to all ages.

4

App and website staying in stepWatch it

Problem: with no internet, a change on the phone can clash with a change made on the website.

Fix: the app keeps a copy and syncs when it is back online. We test the clash case before launch.

5

Using the app with no internetWatch it

Problem: today Reset loads some parts from the internet each time, so a bad connection can open it blank.

Fix: we pack everything it needs into the app so it opens fast and works offline.

6

HV AI and its security checkWatch it

Problem: HV AI only answers requests that pass a check tied to our website address. An app has no website address.

Fix: we switch to each store's own check, which proves the request comes from our real app.

7

Store reviewWatch it

Problem: Apple checks every app by hand and is strict about sign-in, privacy and why we ask for notifications. Google also reviews.

Fix: we follow their rules from the start and expect at least one "please fix this" round on iPhone.

8

Finding the 12 testersPlan ahead

Problem: Google needs 12 people for 14 days. Some forget or leave early.

Fix: ask 15 to 20 people. The Haritt team makes a simple form for their Gmail ids.

9

UpdatesPlan ahead

Problem: once the app is on a phone, website changes do not reach it by themselves.

Fix: small changes can be pushed to the app quietly. Bigger ones go out as a new version through the stores.
