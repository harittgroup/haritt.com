Team guide · 10 minutes

Send emails from your official Haritt email

Emails sent to your official address already arrive in your Gmail. Follow these steps once, and you can also send and reply as firstname.lastname@haritt.com.

Use these exact values in the steps below. Tap Copy to copy any value.

For safety, the password is never on this page or in any link. The team sends it to you as a separate WhatsApp message. Copy it from there when you reach step 5. See Where do I get the password? If you did not get it, write to hr@haritt.com.

Where do I get the password?

  • What it is: a private key that lets Gmail send emails as your official address through our mail server (Resend). It is not your Gmail password and not a new account.
  • Who makes it: the founding team creates one key for each person and sends it to you as a separate WhatsApp message. It looks like re_xxxxxxxx….
  • Keep it private: use it only in step 5. Do not share it, post it or save it in notes. If it leaks, tell us and we will replace it.
  • Did not get it? Write to hr@haritt.com or message the founding team on WhatsApp, and it will be sent to you.
For the founding team: how to create a key

1. Sign in at resend.com and open API Keys.
2. Click Create API Key. Name: the person's name, for example "Aman SMTP".
3. Permission: Sending access. Domain: haritt.com.
4. Click Add, copy the key (it is shown only once) and send it to the person on WhatsApp.
5. To stop it later, delete that one key in API Keys. Nothing else is affected.

Before you start

  • Use a laptop or computer. This setting is only on the Gmail website, not in the Gmail phone app. After setup, sending works on your phone too.
  • Open gmail.com in Chrome and sign in to the Gmail account where your official emails arrive: your personal Gmail.
  • Keep this page open on your phone, so you can read and copy values while you work on the laptop.

No laptop? On your phone, open gmail.com in Chrome, tap the three dots at the top right and tick Desktop site. It works, but it is small and harder.

  1. 1

    Open all settings

    At the top right of Gmail, click the gear icon. A panel opens. Click See all settings.

  2. 2

    Go to "Accounts and Import"

    Settings opens with tabs along the top. Click the tab named Accounts and Import. (In some Gmail accounts it is called Accounts.)

  3. 3

    Click "Add another email address"

    Scroll to the row Send mail as:. Your Gmail address is shown there. Below it, click the blue link Add another email address. A small new window opens.

  4. 4

    Enter your name and official email

    In the new window:

    • Name: Your full name
    • Email address: firstname.lastname@haritt.com
    • Keep Treat as an alias ticked.

    Click Next Step.

  5. 5

    Fill the server details

    Gmail now asks for "SMTP server" details. Copy them from Your details at the top:

    • SMTP Server: smtp.resend.com
    • Port: 465 (choose it from the list)
    • Username: resend (all small letters)
    • Password: the password the team sent you on WhatsApp. Copy and paste it, do not type it.
    • Select Secured connection using SSL.

    Click Add Account.

  6. 6

    Enter the confirmation code

    Gmail sends an email named "Gmail Confirmation - Send Mail as" to firstname.lastname@haritt.com. It arrives in your same Gmail inbox within a minute.

    • Keep the small window open. In your inbox, open that email and copy the number (confirmation code).
    • Paste it in the small window and click Verify.
    • Window closed by mistake? Just click the link in that email. It does the same thing.
  7. 7

    Make it your default and set replies

    Back on Accounts and Import, your official email now shows under Send mail as.

    • Next to it, click make default. New emails will now go from your official email.
    • Under When replying to a message, choose Reply from the same address the message was sent to.

    Gmail saves this by itself. Nothing else to click.

  8. 8

    Send a test email

    Click Compose. In the From line, check it shows firstname.lastname@haritt.com. Send a short "Hi, setup done" to hr@haritt.com. You are done.

    On your phone (Gmail app): open Compose and tap the From line to pick your official email. It may take a few minutes to appear the first time. If it does not, close the app fully and open it again.

  9. 9

    Turn on notifications on your phone

    So you see every email the moment it arrives:

    • Open the Gmail app > tap the three lines (top left) > Settings > tap your Gmail account.
    • Notifications > choose All.
    • In your phone's Settings > Apps (or Notifications) > Gmail, make sure notifications are Allowed.

Rules for your official email

  • This is your one working email for Haritt Group. Every company email goes from it, never from a personal ID.
  • All talk with clients, partners, vendors and the team stays on this email, so the company has a full record.
  • Share only this email on documents, proposals, LinkedIn and anywhere you represent Haritt Group.
  • Reply quickly, the same day at the latest. Keep notifications on.

If something goes wrong

"Authentication failed" or "Couldn't reach server"

Usually the password. Go back, delete the password, and paste it again from the WhatsApp message (no space before or after). Check: server smtp.resend.com, port 465, username resend, SSL selected.

The confirmation email did not come

Wait 2 minutes. Check Spam, Promotions and All Mail in Gmail. Still nothing? In Accounts and Import, next to the address, click Resend email.

I do not see the "Accounts and Import" tab

You are probably in the Gmail phone app. Use a laptop and open gmail.com in Chrome. Settings tabs only show on the website.

My replies still go from my personal Gmail

Do step 7 again: choose Reply from the same address the message was sent to, and make the official email the default.

The From option is missing in the Gmail phone app

Wait a few minutes after setup, then close the Gmail app fully and open it again.

Still stuck

Write to hr@haritt.com with a screenshot of the step where you are stuck.
