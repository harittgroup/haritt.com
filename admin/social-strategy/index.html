3.3

How HV Verse sounds

We are

  • The HV Verse team, writing as "we", like a helpful older friend
  • English for carousels, LinkedIn and YouTube. Natural Hinglish (Roman script) for reels, stories and many Instagram captions
  • Faceless: motion graphics and real app screens, no one on camera
  • Specific: steps, times, examples
  • Honest: free, no login where true, results are for growth, not a diagnosis

We are not

  • Hype: "life-changing", "guaranteed", "100x"
  • Fake urgency or fake numbers
  • Corporate: "leveraging synergies"
  • Emoji walls, all caps or ten hashtags
Before

"Introducing HV Verse, a revolutionary AI ecosystem that will transform your productivity forever! Download now!!"

After

"Missed your plan again? Don't restart tomorrow. Move the rest of today. Here's the 3-step reset we use."

Viral playbook

How HV Verse gets shared, on purpose

Viral is not luck. Posts spread when they make someone save, send or reply. Here is exactly what we post to get that, for Indian students and job seekers.

"I have an answer"

Situation quizzes, "which type are you", rate my plan.

Ends with: "A, B, C or D? Comment."

5.6

Viral, but still us

Do
  • Relatable first, useful second, product last
  • Hinglish hooks for reels, clear English for carousels
  • One idea, made in 3 formats (carousel, reel, Short)
  • Reply to the best comments with a new reel
  • Post the follow-up while the first one is still moving
Never
  • Fake results, fake toppers, fake screenshots
  • Shaming students, parents or teachers
  • Rage bait, politics, religion
  • Trend audio with nothing to learn
  • Copying another creator's post

Growth and performance

How we grow, and how we know it works

Organic first. Useful content, a steady rhythm, real conversations. Measured every Friday.

10.1

How one post gets made

    StepWhoHow
    PlanAnyone in the HV Verse team, SundayPick the week's posts from the calendar and note who takes which
    MakeWhoever took the postWrite with the prompts; design with AI or the templates, then fix it to the brand rules
    CheckA second personRun the checklist below before anything goes out
    Post and replyWhoever is freePost on time, reply to comments and DMs in the first hour
    MeasureAnyone, FridayNote saves, shares, clicks and sign-ups for the week
    Before you post
    • Every fact is true and every feature is real
    • No made-up numbers, users or reviews
    • Brand colours, Sora and Inter, 80 px margins, one product colour
    • Under 25 words a slide, no emojis in the design
    • Link is a tracked link from 10.5
    AI design, done rightAI can make the first draft of a post or a motion graphic. A person always fixes the text, swaps in real app screens and checks it against the brand rules. AI images never show fake app screens or fake people.

    Organic growth, without chasing viral

    Do every week

    • Reply to every comment in the first hour after posting.
    • Leave 10 useful comments a day on study and career creators' posts.
    • One collab post a month with a student creator or a Beta Lab tester.
    • Turn every good question in the DMs into a post.
    • Ask Beta Lab testers to share their own honest posts, never scripted.

    Should we join a trend?

    1Does it fit one of our 5 pillars?
    2Can we add a real tip, not just the joke?
    3Can we make it on-brand in under 2 hours?

    All yes: post itAny no: skip
