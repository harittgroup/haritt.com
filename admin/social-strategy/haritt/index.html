3.3

How Haritt Group sounds

We are

  • The Haritt team, writing as "we": calm, plain, and specific
  • Clear English everywhere, with simple Hinglish only where a reel or story needs it
  • Honest about limits: what we know, what we are still learning
  • Dated and concrete: real years, real products, real examples
  • Useful first: the reader should learn something even if they never click

We are not

  • Corporate: "leveraging synergies", "cutting-edge solutions"
  • Hype: "revolutionary", "guaranteed", "game-changing"
  • Fake numbers, fake clients or fake awards
  • Emoji walls, all caps or hashtag piles
Before

"We are thrilled to announce our cutting-edge AI-driven digital transformation solutions for the modern enterprise!"

After

"AI is not a strategy. It is a tool. Here is one small job it can do in your business this week, and how to check the result."

Growth playbook

How Haritt Group earns shares, on purpose

An authority brand does not chase virality. It earns saves, forwards and replies by being the clearest voice on a topic. Here is how.

5.1

Why people share a post

Our reading
Save

"I'll need this later"

Checklists, plain-English glossaries, step-by-step Playbooks.

Ends with: "Save this for your next project."

Forward

"My co-founder should read this"

Honest lessons and clear opinions a team will want to discuss.

Ends with: "Send this to whoever owns this at your company."

Reply

"I have a view on this"

One real question, asked with no right answer.

Ends with: "What would you add?"

Follow

"I want the next one"

A fixed series with a name and a day.

Ends with: "Next in the series on Thursday."

5.6

Authority, but still us

Do
  • Plain truth first, example second, link last
  • Use real dates, real work and real screens
  • One idea made in 3 formats (PDF carousel, Short, text post)
  • Credit the venture when its work is the example
  • Reply to every thoughtful comment within a day
Never
  • Fake clients, awards or follower counts
  • Attacking competitors by name
  • Hot takes on politics or religion
  • Claims we cannot show a receipt for
  • Copying another company's post

    Organic growth, without chasing viral

    Do every week

    • Reply to every comment in the first hour after posting.
    • Leave 10 thoughtful comments a week on posts by founders and builders.
    • Ask the team to reshare page posts with one line of their own.
    • Turn every good question we get into a post.
    • Publish one Playbooks article a month and build the week around it.
