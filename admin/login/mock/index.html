The one-time proof (already built)

  • You press "Verify my login by email" in Mail > People.
  • A link goes to harsh.goyal@haritt.com, which lands in your forwarding inbox.
  • You open it in the same browser where you are signed in. Done.

What stays awkward

  • Three IDs in your head: login Gmail, company address, forwarding Gmail.
  • The company address cannot sign in anywhere.
  • Every new team member needs the same proof step.

Where mail goes

  • Mail to harsh.goyal@haritt.com still reaches your inbox through Cloudflare, as now.
  • No Gmail inbox is created. Nothing changes for mail.

Your personal Gmail

  • Stays as a backup login at first.
  • Once you trust the new login, we remove it from the allow-list.
  • It remains your private account.

Safer than today

  • 2-step verification is required for everyone.
  • A person who leaves: you switch their ID off and access ends at once.
  • Two super admins, so one lost phone cannot lock you out.

Most of it is automatic. You do three short things on Google's side. I do the rest.

1

YouSign up for Cloud Identity Free

Go to Google's Cloud Identity sign-up. Enter haritt.com. Google gives you a text code to prove you own the domain.

2

I doAdd the proof code to your domain

I put the code in your Cloudflare settings through the config file. You do not touch DNS.

3

YouCreate the accounts

Two super admins (you and Pulkit), then one ID per team member. Turn on 2-step for everyone.

4

I doAdd company sign-in to the admin

The admin accepts @haritt.com Google sign-in. Your personal Gmail keeps working as backup.

5

YouTry it, then switch the old one off

Sign in with the company ID. If it all works, I remove the personal Gmail from the allow-list.

Things to know

  • Google says Free has no Gmail, Calendar or Drive storage, and support is community only.
  • The user limit is not on Google's page. Check it during sign-up.
  • If a normal Google account already exists with a haritt.com address, sign-up may ask you to deal with it first.
