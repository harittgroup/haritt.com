Our story

Why we exist.

Powerful technology is everywhere, but most of it is built for experts. Ordinary people and small teams are left with tools that are complicated, expensive, or both.

Haritt Group was started to change that. We take what AI can do and turn it into products that anyone can open, understand in a minute and use every day: to plan, to learn, to grow and to run their work.

Our first product, HV Verse, brings together a day planner, a career tracker and self-discovery tests, all connected by one assistant that understands plain language. It works in any browser, with nothing to install.

We build for India first, in the languages people actually speak, and for the world next.

Our mission

To make useful technology simple and accessible for everyone, from individuals to growing businesses.

Our vision

To become India’s most trusted home for simple, useful products that people rely on every day.

Our name

Haritt, with two Ts.

How do you spell Haritt, and is it the same as Harit?
Haritt is spelled H-A-R-I-T-T, with two Ts. It is the name of Haritt Group, the company behind Haritt Developers and HV Verse. People sometimes type Harit, Harit Group or Harit Developers with one T. They all mean the same Haritt.

What is Haritt Group?
Haritt Group is an independent technology company in India. It builds simple, useful products with AI and is the home of HV Verse (HV Reset, HV Hunt, HV Assess and HV AI) and Haritt Developers.

Where can I find Haritt Developers and HV Verse?
Haritt Developers, the digital services brand for websites, software, branding, marketing and AI solutions, is at developers.haritt.com. HV Verse, the set of free AI apps, is at thehvverse.com.
