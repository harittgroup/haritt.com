About

Why we exist.

Powerful technology is everywhere, but most of it is built for experts. Ordinary people and small teams are left with tools that are complicated, expensive, or both.

Haritt Group was started to change that. We turn what technology and AI can do into things anyone can open, understand in a minute and use every day.

Haritt started in 2020 as a small web development project and was relaunched in 2026 as a group that takes on real problems for people and businesses, through its own ventures, brands and solutions.

We build for India first, in the languages people actually speak, and for the world next.

Our mission

To solve real problems for people and businesses everywhere, with technology that is simple, useful and within everyone’s reach.

Our vision

A world where every person and every business, anywhere, can use the best of technology to live and work better.
