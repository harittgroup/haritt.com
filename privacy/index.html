Privacy

Privacy notice.

How haritt.com handles visits and the details you share with Haritt Group.

What we collect

haritt.com has no accounts and no forms. If you write to us by email, we receive your email address and your message, and use them only to reply. We don't sell your information or add you to marketing lists without asking.

How we count visits

We use two tools to understand which pages help people:

Our own simple counter. It counts numbers per day, such as page views, how a visit arrived and device type, with no cookies, names or IDs. Only the Haritt team can see the totals.

Google Analytics. It uses first-party cookies to measure visits, pages viewed and how people move through the site. We have turned off Google's advertising features and Google signals, so it is not used for ads. Google processes this data under the Google Privacy Policy.

Browsers that send Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control are never counted by either tool. You can also turn counting off on this device or turn it back on, or use Google's opt-out browser add-on.

Other services

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which receives technical data such as your IP address to deliver and secure the site. Fonts load from Google Fonts. A small script from thehvverse.com shows planned maintenance notices.

Your choices

You can ask us to show, correct or delete your information at any time by writing to contact@haritt.com.
